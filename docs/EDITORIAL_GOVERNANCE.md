# Editorial Governance & Source Hierarchy — Rang & Riwaayat

## 1. Editorial Workflow Stages
1. **Draft:** Initial research brief drafted by cultural writers.
2. **Fact Check:** Verification of historical dates, archaeological citations, and names.
3. **Cultural Review:** Verification with regional community scholars to avoid stereotyping or homogenization.
4. **Translation:** Human localized translation into Hindi and Gujarati.
5. **Language Review:** Indic script typographical and grammatical inspection.
6. **Approved:** Ready for scheduling.
7. **Published:** Live on public website and indexed in sitemap.

## 2. Institutional Source Hierarchy
We accept primary documentation from:
1. Positional Astronomy Centre (IMD) / Rashtriya Panchang.
2. Archaeological Survey of India (ASI).
3. Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts (IGNCA).
4. Sangeet Natak Akademi (SNA).
5. UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage Committee.
6. Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee (SGPC).
7. Central Ruet-e-Hilal Committees and Shahi Imam archives.
