# Rang & Riwaayat — Project Overview
**Tagline:** Celebrate Every Tradition.

## 1. Executive Summary
**Rang & Riwaayat** is a production-grade, culturally authentic, multilingual digital platform dedicated to discovering, understanding, preparing for, and celebrating the vast tapestry of Indian festivals, traditions, regional arts, rituals, and cuisine.

Unlike static demo sites, superficial calendar scrapers, or AI-hallucinated cultural portals, Rang & Riwaayat is built with rigorous editorial standards, verifiable source hierarchies, verified regional calendar occurrences (tithi, muhurat, regional variations), and deep interactive functionality including client-side high-resolution card design, an interactive India culture explorer with achievement passports, cooking assistants with dynamic ingredient calculators, and a real-time multiplayer cultural game arena.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Cultural Authenticity & Respect:** Full parity and dignified coverage across Hindu, Muslim, Sikh, Christian, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi/Zoroastrian, tribal, regional, and secular Indian traditions without ranking or homogenization.
2. **Multilingual Architecture:** First-class localized experiences in English (`/en`), Hindi (`/hi`), and Gujarati (`/gu`), with localized typography, Indic font support, localized URL slugs, canonical links, and hreflang metadata.
3. **Verified Knowledge System:** Every festival occurrence, ritual, and claim links to authentic institutional sources (Government cultural ministries, Sangeet Natak Akademi, Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts, authentic community institutes, academic research).
4. **Interactive Celebration Ecosystem:**
   - **Celebration Studio:** HTML5 Canvas-based high-res greeting card maker with custom Indian motifs, bilingual typography, and instant export/share without forced signups.
   - **Culture Explorer & Passport:** Interactive vector map of India covering all 28 states & 8 UTs with regional arts, music, dance, rituals, and passport collection.
   - **Interactive Festival Calendar:** Month, state, and community filtering, regional variation toggle, ICS export, and countdowns.
   - **Multiplayer Fun Zone:** Real-time authoritative game rooms powered by WebSockets/Socket.IO featuring Festival Battle, Guess the Festival, Food Detective, Bharat Map Challenge, and Desi Party Trivia.
   - **Recipe Companion:** Dynamic serving multiplier, interactive step checklists, and cooking timers.
5. **Private Administrative Governance:** Secure role-based management dashboard for multi-stage editorial workflows (Draft -> Research -> Fact Check -> Cultural Review -> Translation -> Approved -> Published), correction tracking, and asset licensing.
