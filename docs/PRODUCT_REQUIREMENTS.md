# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Rang & Riwaayat
**Tagline:** Celebrate Every Tradition.

## 1. Scope & Functional Requirements
Rang & Riwaayat provides complete end-to-end user journeys across:
1. **Multilingual Editorial Platform:**
   - English (`/en`), Hindi (`/hi`), Gujarati (`/gu`).
   - Sourced and verified across all major Indian religions and tribal communities.
   - Comprehensive detail pages with history, rituals, recipes, decoration, and FAQs.
2. **Interactive India Culture Explorer:**
   - All 28 States and 8 Union Territories represented.
   - Interactive regional filtering (North, South, East, West, Central, North-East).
   - Culture Passport stamp collection saved locally.
3. **Celebration Studio (Card Maker):**
   - Editable greeting cards with custom recipient and sender names.
   - Live canvas rendering with Indic fonts.
   - Local high-resolution PNG export and Web Share API.
4. **Realtime Multiplayer Arena:**
   - Authoritative Socket.IO game engine.
   - Private rooms with PIN and password.
   - Synchronized question timers and live leaderboards.
5. **Interactive Recipe Guide:**
   - Serving size multiplier with automatic ingredient scaling.
   - Interactive step checklists and built-in countdown cooking timers.
6. **Community Governance:**
   - Public correction and observation submission form.
   - Private administrator review queue.
