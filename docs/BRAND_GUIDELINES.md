# Brand Guidelines — Rang & Riwaayat
**Brand:** RANG & RIWAAYAT  
**Tagline:** Celebrate Every Tradition.

## 1. Palette Design Tokens
| Token Name | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Vermilion | `#8F3026` | Primary brand color, headers, key action CTAs |
| Antique Gold | `#D6A84B` | Ornate accents, badges, borders, celebratory motifs |
| Warm Ivory | `#F6EBDD` | Warm paper/silk background surfaces |
| Charcoal | `#24201D` | High contrast editorial text, footer background |

## 2. Typography
- **Headings (English):** Editorial serif (`Merriweather`, `Cinzel`, `Playfair Display`).
- **Body Text:** Modern neutral sans-serif (`Inter`).
- **Hindi (Devanagari):** `Noto Sans Devanagari`.
- **Gujarati:** `Noto Sans Gujarati`.

## 3. Cultural Motifs
All iconography and decorative accents (Diyas, Mandalas, Kalash, Crescent, Lotus) are rendered as crisp, scalable vector graphics, avoiding excessive neon gradients or generic stock textures.
