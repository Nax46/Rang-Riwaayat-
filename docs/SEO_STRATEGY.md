# SEO & Organic Traffic Strategy — Rang & Riwaayat

## 1. Multilingual URL Architecture
- `/en/festivals/diwali`
- `/hi/festivals/diwali`
- `/gu/festivals/diwali`

Every localized route contains:
- Self-referencing canonical tag.
- Complete `hreflang` alternate link tags for English, Hindi, and Gujarati.
- Distinct localized OpenGraph metadata.

## 2. Structured Data (Schema.org)
- Festival Pages: `Article` + `BreadcrumbList`.
- Recipes: `Recipe` with cooking times, prep times, ingredients, and yield.
- Calendar: `Event` markup only when specific public processions or temple schedules meet Google search guidelines.

## 3. Seasonal Publishing Timeline
- **60–45 days prior:** Comprehensive festival guides and historical significance.
- **45–30 days prior:** Regional observance differences and tithi alerts.
- **30–15 days prior:** Festive recipes, prasad guides, and home decor ideas.
- **15–7 days prior:** Greeting cards, wishes, WhatsApp statuses, and digital invitations.
- **Festival Day:** Real-time muhurat updates and social sharing.
- **Post Festival:** Evergreen folklore and photographic retrospectives.
